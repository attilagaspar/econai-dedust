Step 1 · erase table rules
Min rule length 22%
A dark run spanning at least this % of the page is treated as a table rule and painted white. Lower = shorter rules also erased. Higher = only full-width/height rules removed.
Step 2 · binarise (paper → white, ink → black)
Instead of one global cutoff, each pixel is compared to its local neighbourhood. Handles faded ink and uneven paper tone. Uncheck to skip binarisation entirely.
Neighbourhood size 15
Pixel window used to compute the local mean (must be odd). Roughly match the height of one digit in pixels. Too small → noisy; too large → loses faint ink.
Sensitivity (C) 10
Subtracted from the local mean before thresholding. Higher = more pixels become white (aggressive background removal, may clip faint digits). Lower = more pixels kept black (preserves faint ink, but also more noise).
Step 3 · remove speckle noise
Noise removal radius 2
Erases isolated black specks smaller than this radius (paper grain, dust). 0 = off. Too large = thin digit strokes disappear.
